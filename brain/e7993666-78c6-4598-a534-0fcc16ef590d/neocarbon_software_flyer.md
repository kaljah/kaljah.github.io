     $$\text{Tier 1 (IPCC Defaults)} \longrightarrow \text{Tier 2 (Basin / Regional Factors)} \longrightarrow \text{Tier 3 (Thermodynamic Process Physics)}$$
   - **Source-Level Detail**: Full unbroken lineage from equipment to corporate rollup:
     $$\text{Wellhead / Component} \longrightarrow \text{Battery / Facility} \longrightarrow \text{Enterprise Rollup}$$
3. **What Makes NeoCarbon Different (The 6 Engineering Principles)**:
   - **01 / Calculation**: Engineering formulas driven by fluid composition, operating pressure, and physical properties.
   - **02 / Factors**: Flexible hierarchy cascading from custom corporate factors down to API, regional, and IPCC tables.
   - **03 / Traceability**: Strict input $\rightarrow$ calculation $\rightarrow$ result preservation for complete auditor review.
   - **04 / Methane**: OGMP 2.0 Level 4/5 workflows reconciling bottom-up component physics with top-down measurement (drones, aircraft, satellites).
   - **05 / Composition**: Custom fuel & gas chromatography (C1–C6+, $\text{CO}_2$, $\text{N}_2$, $\text{H}_2\text{S}$) calculating constituent-specific mass emissions.
   - **06 / Control**: Immutable audit version history and change logs ensuring 100% reproducibility.

   $$\text{Input Data} \xrightarrow{\text{Unit Normalization}} \text{Tier Dispatcher} \xrightarrow{\text{Stoichiometry / EOS}} \text{Speciated GHG } (\text{CO}_2, \text{CH}_4, \text{N}_2\text{O}) \xrightarrow{\text{GWP Horizons}} \text{Total } \text{CO}_2\text{e}$$
