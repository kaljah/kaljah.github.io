The **"Compendium of Greenhouse Gas Emissions Methodologies for the Oil and Natural Gas Industry"** (2021 edition) is a foundational reference document published by the American Petroleum Institute (API)[1].

While I cannot directly provide the PDF file for download, you can access the document through the following official API resource:

*   **Official Link:** [API 2021 GHG Compendium](https://www.api.org/-/media/Files/Policy/ESG/GHG/2021-API-GHG-Compendium-110921.pdf)[1]

### Key Information about this Document:
*   **Purpose:** It provides standardized methodologies and emission factors for estimating greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions across oil and natural gas industry operations[1]. It is widely used by operators and governments to establish emissions baselines and report Scope 1, Scope 2, and sometimes Scope 3 emissions[1][2][3].
*   **Context:** This 2021 version represents the fourth update to the original document, which was first published in 2001[1]. It is frequently cited in corporate sustainability reports, CDP questionnaires, and regulatory filings[2][4][3][5].
*   **Related Tools:** The methodologies in the Compendium are integrated into various industry tools, such as the **SANGEA™** software, which is designed by the API to assist companies in calculating their GHG inventories[2].
