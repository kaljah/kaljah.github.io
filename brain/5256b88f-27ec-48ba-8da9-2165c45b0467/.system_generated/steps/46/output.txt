*   **Table 6-10:** Typically titled *"Well Unloading Vented Emission Factors for Wells"*[1][2].

These tables provide standardized emission factors used by the oil and gas industry to estimate methane and other greenhouse gas emissions resulting from the practice of liquids unloading—a process used to remove accumulated liquids from gas wells to maintain production[1][4][2].
