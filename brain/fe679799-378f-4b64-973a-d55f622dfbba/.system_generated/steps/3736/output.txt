The Hassi R'Mel gas field is Algeria's largest gas producer[1][2]. Its gas composition is characterized by a high methane content, typically ranging between **77% and 82% mole fraction**[3][4].

### Typical Gas Composition (Hassi R'Mel)

### PCS (Gross Calorific Value) and ISO 6976
*   **PCS (Pouvoir Calorifique Supérieur):** In the oil and gas industry, the *Pouvoir Calorifique Supérieur* (Gross Calorific Value) represents the total energy released upon complete combustion of the gas[7][8]. Because Hassi R'Mel gas is rich in methane with significant fractions of heavier hydrocarbons (ethane, propane, butanes), it possesses a substantial calorific value, making it highly suitable for export and industrial use[3][4].
*   **ISO 6976 Standard:** Sonatrach and other major industry players utilize the **ISO 6976** international standard to calculate the thermodynamic properties of natural gas[9][8]. This standard provides the rigorous mathematical framework required to determine:
    *   Gross and Net Calorific Values (PCS/PCI)[10][11].
    *   Density and Relative Density[10][8].
    *   Wobbe Index[10][8].

These calculations are performed using the molar composition of the gas as input[10]. If you are performing these calculations, ensure that your molar fractions are normalized to 100% and that the reference conditions (e.g., 15°C/15°C or 0°C/0°C) align with your specific contractual or regulatory requirements[10][12][13].
