### Content and Context
*   **Purpose:** This table typically provides **vented methane (CH4) emission factors** for drilling mud degassing, which is the process of extracting entrained gas from drilling mud after it returns from the wellbore[2][4][3].
*   **Units:** The emission factors in these tables are often expressed in terms of **tonnes of CH4 per drilling day** or **pounds (lb) of total hydrocarbons (THC) per drilling day**, depending on the specific edition and methodology document[4].
*   **Variations by Mud Base:** Table 6-2 often categorizes these emission factors by the type of mud used, such as[4]:

    *   **Synthetic-based mud**[4]
