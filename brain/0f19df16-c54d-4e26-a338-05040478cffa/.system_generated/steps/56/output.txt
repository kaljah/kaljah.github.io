The values **0.2605** and **0.0586** (measured in metric tonnes of $CH_4$ per drilling day) are emission factors used for **mud degassing** in oil and natural gas operations[1][2][3].

*   **0.2605**: Factor for **water-based** drilling muds[4][5][3].
*   **0.0586**: Factor for **oil-based** and **synthetic** drilling muds[4][5][3].

### Context in API Compendium and Regulations
These factors are widely referenced in industry and regulatory documentation:
*   **API Compendium:** These values appear in the *Compendium of Greenhouse Gas Emissions Methodologies for the Oil and Natural Gas Industry* (API Compendium)[1][3]. While different editions of the Compendium use various table numbers for different data (e.g., Table 6-2 often refers to "Facility-Level Average Fugitive Emission Factors"[6][7][8]), the specific emission factors for mud degassing are standard benchmarks derived from industry studies[2][9].
*   **Regulatory Usage (EPA Subpart W):** These specific values (0.2605 for water-based, 0.0586 for oil-based/synthetic) are codified in **40 CFR Part 98 Subpart W** (Petroleum and Natural Gas Systems) as the default emission factors for calculating vented methane emissions from drilling mud degassing[4][5]. 

If you are looking for "Table 6-2" specifically within the API Compendium, please note that table numbering can vary between versions (e.g., the 2009 edition vs. the 2021 edition)[10][11].
