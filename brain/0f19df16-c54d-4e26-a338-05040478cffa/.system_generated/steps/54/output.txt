Table 6-2 in the **API Compendium of Greenhouse Gas Emissions Methodologies for the Oil and Natural Gas Industry** is titled **"Mud Degassing Vented Methane Emission Factors for Oil and Natural Gas Exploration."**[1]

This table provides standardized emission factors used to estimate methane emissions resulting from the degassing of drilling mud during exploration activities.[1] These factors are typically categorized to account for the different characteristics of the fluids being processed—specifically distinguishing between **water-based** and **oil-based** drilling mud systems—as the gas content and release potential can vary significantly between them.
