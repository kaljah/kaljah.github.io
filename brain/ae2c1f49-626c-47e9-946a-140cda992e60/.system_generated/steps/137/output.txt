A1: (Recommended) Net-Vented Mass Balance: Compute total associated gas (GOR × Oil Production), deduct recovered/conserved volume and flared volume to isolate net vented gas (V_vented = Total - Recovered - Flared, or via Venting Duration / Vent Fraction). Only the net vented stream generates emissions for this record, while reporting flared/recovered volumes in metadata to ensure zero double-counting with Section 5 flaring.
