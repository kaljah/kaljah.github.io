Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything technical, IT security, and executive directors need to know about implementing NeoCarbon.

How does NeoCarbon differ from generic corporate ESG platforms?
Generic ESG tools rely on spend-based economic averages that produce massive errors when applied to oil & gas assets. NeoCarbon is an engineering-first platform that uses true chemical stoichiometry, gas chromatography molar splits, higher heating values, and the API Compendium 2021 Tier 3 methodology.
Can NeoCarbon be deployed on-premise in air-gapped sovereign environments?
Yes. We recognize that national energy companies and upstream operators have strict data sovereignty requirements. NeoCarbon can be deployed fully on-premise within your secure datacenter, on private government clouds, or via containerized Docker/Kubernetes clusters with zero external internet dependencies.
How does the Maker-Checker workflow protect against audit penalties?
NeoCarbon enforces the 4-eyes principle. Field operators or automated SCADA feeds stage data as "Pending Review". A certified emissions checker must review, validate, and electronically sign the records before they commit to the official regulatory ledger. Every change is cryptographically hashed with SHA-256 for non-repudiation.
How easy is it to migrate our existing Excel emission spreadsheets?
Extremely rapid. NeoCarbon includes an intelligent column-mapping ingestion wizard that auto-detects date formats, fuel types, activity units (Sm³, MMSCF, boe, MWh), and facility IDs. Typical historical data ingestion for 5 years of field data takes less than 48 hours.
Does NeoCarbon support EU CBAM cross-border export declarations?
Yes. NeoCarbon includes a dedicated CBAM export module configured to calculate specific embedded direct and indirect emissions per metric ton of exported hydrocarbon product (LNG, LPG, refinery distillates, fertilizers) matching the transitional and definitive European Commission CBAM registries.
